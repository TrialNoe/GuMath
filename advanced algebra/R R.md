> [!question] Example 0
> A must have 5 rows and 4 columns. For the domain of T to be $R^{ 4 }$ , A must have four columns so that $A\mathbf{x}$ is defined for $\mathbf{x}$ in $R^{ 4 }$ . For the codomain of T to be $R^{ 5 }$ , the columns of A must have five entries (in which case A must have five rows), because $A\mathbf{x}$ is a linear combination of the columns of A.
